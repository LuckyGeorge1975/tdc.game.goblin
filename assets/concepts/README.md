# Unit Art Direction Studies

Preview concepts only. These images define shape language, material treatment and palette; they are not yet optimized game assets.

1. `01-modular-stealth-geometry.png` — modular wedges, chamfered armor, graphite/petrol with cyan and lime light accents.
2. `02-industrial-exoframe.png` — exposed rectangular rails, replaceable modules, steel blue-gray with safety orange.
3. `03-monolithic-facet.png` — large faceted armor masses, minimal detail, black-green and titanium with amber-red light bars.
4. `04-autonomous-drone-corps.png` — flat unmanned platform, rectangular sensor arrays, warm gray and graphite with electric blue.
5. `05-aerospace-ground-force.png` — arrowhead ground vehicle, cropped fins and recessed track pods, gunmetal and broken white with coral red.

Shared prompt constraints: original autonomous heavy ground vehicle; centered isometric three-quarter top-down view; dark neutral hex-floor studio; premium hard-surface 3D concept render; predominantly straight lines; very few curves; no crew, logos, letters, text or watermark; no retro or classic cybertank styling.
