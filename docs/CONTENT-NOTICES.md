# Content & Asset Notices

**Document version:** 9

G.O.B.L.I.N. is an independent original prototype by Tactical Design Cell. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or presented as an official adaptation of any third-party game or product line.

Build 0.1.17 uses code-generated SVG and CSS artwork plus six original, locally hosted unit-art styles created for this project. Five styles serve smaller Content-08 WebP Unit Guide views with PNG fallback; the sixth uses larger SVG symbols. The active style can be changed in the game. Locally available system fonts are used; no third-party image, audio, or webfont package is required by that content layer.

Each of the six styles contains 26 small transparent SVG icons for the map and unit lists. The style selector changes icons and Guide views together. Five styles are project-authored graphical designs with 26 Content-08 Guide views each; the sixth uses military map-symbol vocabulary and presents the same tactical symbol motif in separate small and large SVG files. The icons and military symbols were drawn by local generators from the project's unit manifest and design instructions. None of the Content-08 per-unit Guide views are pixel-identical crops of the concept sheets. The game loads assets from local project paths.

Five separate, AI-generated PNG concept sheets helped compare the graphical styles. Each sheet shows four example units. The concept sheets and their prompts are documented in the Unit-Art source checkout; they are not the 26 per-unit views loaded by the game. If the source proposal files are later published or served as static files, their AI origin and review-only role remain relevant. The user supplied a standing soldier silhouette as a visual reference for the five graphical SVG styles. The source image and its pixels are not bundled in these unit-art sets. The user stated that their inputs were authorized and, after comparing the selected technical style with the reference, reported no concrete similarity. The other four styles share locally drawn vector geometry and were reviewed as part of the asset assessment; the source image's rights were not independently verified here.

Build 0.1.17 includes 130 AI-generated transparent Unit Guide views: 26 individual views for each of the five graphical styles. They use the project's internal content-05 concept sheets as style references, with newly interpreted per-unit details rather than crops or pixel-identical extracts. The concept sheets and the user's reference image are not bundled in these runtime Guide sets. Prompts, image-by-image provenance, alpha and bounds checks, and human visual review are recorded in `tdc.game.ogre-unit-art/assets/unit-art/proposals/content-08/README.md` at source commit `10131dc16ede760f2338b6605e10675f0cd81d29`. The smaller transparent WebP views and PNG fallbacks come from those unchanged masters; they do not introduce new artwork. AI-generated details may not be unique. The asset-level review found no specific obstacle to a noncommercial public preview with this notice; it does not establish rights to third-party inputs, guarantee uniqueness, or clear the complete game for publication. The technical, content and legal release gates and the deployment evidence must be recorded in `goblin-hr7`; the user's authorization for this release request is already recorded there.

The sixth style is an **unofficial original symbol set** inspired by familiar tactical-map conventions. It is not an official NATO symbol library and makes no APP-6 or MIL-STD-2525 conformance claim. The limited asset review found no specific obstacle to a noncommercial preview of these six sets and concept sheets, but it is not a product, trademark, rules, or commercial-use clearance. The 0.1.16 release received separate user authorization, and the 0.1.17 release request is authorized in `goblin-hr7`. Technical, content, and legal decisions for the full game remain required before publication.

Build 0.1.15 also adds four project-authored Legacy showcase levels and localized objective and briefing text. They use historical Field Test rules and provisional unit values. Their inclusion does not claim that planned catalog rules or special abilities are implemented.

The modular terrain preview offers four interchangeable styles: Verdant Atlas, Dryland Survey, Natural Terrain, and Field Atlas. They share one locally authored VisualMap. Their world-aligned forest details are locally generated transparent SVG files; Verdant and Dryland also use SVG ground details, while Natural and Field Atlas use locally generated transparent RGBA-PNG ground details. These terrain files are bundled under `assets/terrain-map-study/portable/` and are loaded through local project paths.

Field Atlas takes broad terrain motifs from a map image supplied by the user, including light grassland, sandy elevations, dark woodland, yellow fields, and pale roads. Its paths, palette, and textures were generated in this project; the supplied image file and its pixels are not bundled in the terrain package. The user confirmed that the generated result has no concrete visual similarity to the reference and that the inputs for generated graphics were authorized and contained no third-party reference images used without rights. The resulting low-risk assessment for a noncommercial preview, its reliance on that user comparison, and its limits are recorded in [the terrain preview clearance supplement](../reports/CONTENT-CLEARANCE-TERRAIN-PREVIEW-2026-09-30.md). This provenance note is not a binding legal or product clearance.
